Modified: first second, grayscale, contrast increased, nearest-neighbor resize to 320x240, original noise audio removed.

Bundled asset is used only when the TV-static transition is selected.

Layered intro score (music_version=2)
intro-studio.ogg is an original, locally composed 16-second stereo score.
Rebuild with: .venv/bin/python tools/generate_intro_score.py
Layered extended chords, FM glass-key melody, rounded bass, quiet percussion,
filtered-noise entrance textures and stereo room taps. No external samples,
recordings, downloads, or paid generation API are used. Licensed with this project.
Each render trims to the saved card duration and fades out before original speech.
